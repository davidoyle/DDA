# Get deep into the problem. Find what matters.

DDA investigates hard public, industrial, regulated, and institutional problems. We rebuild how the system works and follow the evidence across sources and organizations. Then we find the constraint, gap, dependency, assumption, or exposure that shapes the outcome.

Next we find what the finding changes, and we build the analysis that the situation needs. The product can be a model, a register, a map, a strategy, a brief, or a decision tool. The work starts with the problem, even when the question is still forming.

## Six kinds of analysis

Most problems need more than one.

**Evidence reconstruction.** We trace each figure to its primary source and record what it can and cannot show.

**Economic and market analysis.** We measure what an action adds, what it moves from another place, and what leaves the region.

**Fiscal and scenario modelling.** We connect growth, cost, timing, and revenue, then test which assumption carries the result.

**Labour and workforce analysis.** We divide a workforce total down to the supply that a project can use.

**Land, infrastructure, and spatial analysis.** We screen land against every condition between a designation and a completed project.

**Institutional and policy analysis.** We follow public claims and decisions to their sources and explain why an institution produced its result.

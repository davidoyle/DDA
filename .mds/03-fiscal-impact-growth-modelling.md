# Fiscal impact and growth modelling

## What must be true before the target enters the budget?

A population target, land-demand estimate, servicing request, or revenue forecast can reach council or a board before anyone has shown what must be true for delivery. DDA helps decision-makers see the assumptions inside the headline and the conditions that could change the answer.

**Open the number behind the target**

## The consequential decision

The decision may be whether to approve a growth target, fund infrastructure, change a policy, accept a fiscal exposure, or commission better evidence first. Each choice depends on a different unit: a household, parcel, service area, cost category, or period.

## The controlling condition

Theoretical land capacity is different from realistic capacity. Housing need is different from construction output. Public revenue is different from net fiscal position. The controlling condition may sit in servicing, operating cost, market absorption, employment, timing, or the definition of the source number itself.

DDA connects growth to housing, employment, land, infrastructure, operating cost, and public revenue so the constraint is visible before the target becomes a commitment.

## The analysis

DDA builds a capacity reconciliation and assumptions register. Each material input has a source, date, unit, horizon, and status. ACTUAL identifies a named, dated source. PROXY identifies an informative comparison that differs from the target. FLAG identifies a material gap, its consequence, and the route to resolve it.

The model compares a base case with policy-adjusted or sensitivity cases. It shows which assumption carries the result, how far the result moves, and which input deserves confirmation before approval.

## The usable output

The work can produce a model, capacity reconciliation, scenario matrix, assumptions register, sensitivity table, technical appendix, and decision brief. The brief brings the choice, trade-off, owner, date, and unresolved condition into one place.

The decision-maker can revise the target, change a delivery condition, obtain the missing evidence, or proceed with a clearly stated exposure.

Current law, tax treatment, engineering conditions, and client data require current or specialist review. DDA does not provide tax, legal, engineering, investment, or securities advice.

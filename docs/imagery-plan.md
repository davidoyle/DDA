# Imagery plan

## Families and prompts
1. **Places under decision:** wide documentary view of a Metro Vancouver growth edge, subdued overcast light, infrastructure and terrain legible, negative space; avoid dramatic tourism grading.
2. **Systems at work:** working port, utility corridor, construction logistics or transit interface in Canadian context; people incidental rather than posed.
3. **People responsible for decisions:** candid public-sector workshop or site review with documents visible; no handshakes, staged smiles or invented DDA staff.
4. **Evidence made visible:** close editorial view of maps, marked-up policy, model sheets and evidence registers; remove confidential identifiers.
5. **Community and economic context:** main street, industrial land, housing and work in one geographic story; natural subdued light.

Use licensed assets only and record source, licence, subject, place, date and crop. Generated-image prompts must request documentary realism and must never depict a real client engagement. The current abstract systems artwork is decorative and requires empty alt text / `aria-hidden`.

## Alt text
Describe the decision-relevant content and place, not aesthetics. Do not start with “image of.” Decorative textures use empty alt. Charts need a short alt plus an adjacent full text finding; complex maps need a long description. Never infer identity, disability, role or relationship.

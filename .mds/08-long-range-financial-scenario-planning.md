# Long-range financial and scenario planning

A fiscal, tax, royalty, investment, capital, or long-range model can produce a precise result without revealing which assumption carries it. DDA opens the model, traces the evidence, and tests how timing and uncertainty change the answer.

## Find what makes the result move

The important issue may be source vintage, legal treatment, pass-through, behaviour, cost escalation, project timing, demand, or one unresolved input. DDA documents each material assumption with its source, definition, horizon, sensitivity, and effect on the choice.

A base case is tested beside credible alternatives. Sensitivity and breakeven work show which inputs materially change the range and which uncertainties have little effect.

## Build scenarios that answer a live question

The analysis can combine defined planning horizons and deterministic modelling with bottom-up fiscal analysis, revenue and cost modules, reconciliation, behavioural assumptions, and business-impact framing. Current laws, rates, and official figures are checked for the work at hand.

The goal is a model whose logic can be inspected, updated, and explained. Decision-makers can see where judgment enters and what new evidence would justify a different course.

## Deliverables shaped to the users

Work may include model architecture, base and alternative scenarios, a source and assumptions register, sensitivity and breakeven tables, range analysis, technical documentation, an executive brief, and handoff notes.

The analysis does not substitute for specialist legal, tax, engineering, investment, or securities advice.

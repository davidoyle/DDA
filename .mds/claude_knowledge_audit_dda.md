## 2. The analytical method

### Data Lexicon / evidence register

Claude repeatedly describes the register as DDA’s core intellectual property:

- **ACTUAL:** primary-sourced, verified, named, dated information.
- **PROXY:** a disclosed derived value or comparable benchmark, with its basis shown.
- **FLAG:** a known unresolved gap, with what is missing, why it matters, how it could be resolved, and the consequence if it remains unresolved.

The register is not treated as a formatting device. Claude’s later synthesis describes it as an evidentiary or litigant’s discipline: concede the gap before an opposing reader finds it, and prevent an unsupported number from quietly becoming a conclusion.

Repeated register characteristics include source provenance, vintage/date, confidence codes, resolution paths, responsible parties, deadlines, and consequence statements. Register counts are used as evidence of pre-engagement work.

Examples recorded in conversations include:

- Fruitvale: 91 ACTUAL / 10 PROXY / 21 FLAG in one version.
- CCRD: 65 ACTUAL / 35 PROXY / 9 FLAG.
- Parksville: later expanded to 83 ACTUAL / 14 PROXY / 20 FLAG, 117 total entries.
- RDBN: 243-row register.
- Lytton: a nine-part register with eight confidence/status codes and P1–P4 priorities.

### Repeated analytical operations

- Build or assemble the evidence base before adopting a preferred framework.
- Read across years, departments, agencies, and document types.
- Compare stated institutional outcomes with observable system behaviour.
- Quantify structural gaps instead of merely describing them.
- Identify the constraint that binds first, not only the opportunity that looks largest.
- Disaggregate aggregate categories into non-substitutable decision units.
- Refuse comparisons when definitions or populations do not align.
- Treat absence as evidence of non-verifiability, without claiming that absence proves concealment or falsity.
- Use bounded ranges and scenario structures when point estimates would overstate certainty.
- Present findings as explicit choices and trade-offs, not abstract recommendations.
- Build outputs to withstand council questions, procurement challenge, public hearing scrutiny, legal cross-examination, or adversarial review.

### Method shapes that Claude distinguished

Claude’s August method discussion corrected an earlier flat list of principles. It recognized that different problems require different architectures even when the shared register remains constant:

- **Existing-number / fiscal work:** decompose an official figure, test its assumptions, reconcile a range to the official number.
- **Unknown-cost / attrition work:** prevent double-counting, separate mutually exclusive categories, and quarantine scenarios that cannot be safely subtotalled.
- **Labour-shortage work:** disaggregate total headcount into trades, timing, geographic competition, and substitution limits.
- **Institutional-response work:** trace events, detection lag, responsibility, communication, and implementation failure.
- **Planning work:** connect demographic, housing, infrastructure, employment, land, and policy assumptions in sequence.

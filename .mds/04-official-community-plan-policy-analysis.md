# Official Community Plan review and policy analysis

## Can the policy carry the decision at the hearing?

An OCP review, zoning amendment, housing response, or housekeeping package can be coherent on paper and still leave a practical question unanswered: what evidence, mechanism, and delivery condition connect the policy to its stated result?

**Test the plan before the hearing**

## The consequential decision

Council and staff may need to decide which policies should advance, what needs revision, which options belong in public engagement, and what remains open before adoption or amendment.

## The controlling condition

A policy objective is not evidence that the objective can be delivered. A permitted use is not deliverable capacity. A housing target can sit apart from land, servicing, infrastructure, construction economics, and the local market.

The controlling condition is the point where policy depends on a fact, mechanism, or delivery capacity that has not been established.

## The analysis

DDA builds a policy-to-delivery trace. For each material policy, the analysis asks:

1. What does the policy say?
2. What evidence supports the stated condition?
3. What mechanism is expected to produce the result?
4. Which delivery condition must be present?
5. What remains unresolved before the choice?

The analysis can combine a comprehensive policy revision, targeted zoning changes, primary-source research, an evidence-status register, and material for council and public-hearing scrutiny. It keeps policy choices, factual findings, and unresolved delivery conditions separate.

## The usable output

A scoped review can produce an OCP-to-zoning crosswalk, annotated bylaw, policy alignment matrix, evidence register, housing and land findings, policy options, engagement material, and a hearing brief.

Council can see where it is making a policy choice and where that choice relies on a delivery condition. Staff can assign the missing evidence and explain the issue in language that can withstand public scrutiny.

This is policy and evidence analysis, not a legal opinion or compliance certification. Statutory requirements and current local conditions require current review.

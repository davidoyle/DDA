# Public content architecture

## User archetypes
| Archetype | Goal | Likely entry | Minimum trust/content needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Municipal executive | Make a defensible growth, fiscal, land or infrastructure choice | Capability, work story, housing insight | Controlling conditions, sources, scenarios, usable instrument |
| Public-policy leader | Understand policy mechanism and implementation risk | Insight, institutional analysis | Claim/source separation, unknowns, accountability |
| Labour or workforce leader | Establish usable supply and timing risk | Labour capability, trade-gap insight | Decomposition, qualifications, timing, limitations |
| Resource/major-project leader | Protect a consequential schedule | Resource capability, selected work | Dependency chain, sensitivity, owner and date |
| Adviser/researcher | Assess the analysis before referral or collaboration | Article, about, legal | Clear scope, evidence standard, caveats, principal-led model |

## Sitemap
Home branches to What we do (seven capability details), Selected work, Insights (three articles), Who we are, Search, and Contact. Privacy, Legal, Terms, and Accessibility sit in the global footer. Diagnostic tools remain a distinct `/tools/*` application family.

## Primary flows
1. **Problem-led:** search/deep link → capability diagnosis and evidence module → related work/insight → Discuss a decision.
2. **Proof-led:** home/work → verified question, analysis, shift and instrument → relevant capability → contact.
3. **Insight-led:** article → related capability/insight → contact.
4. **Referral validation:** home → who we are → selected work/evidence standard → contact.
5. **Tool-led:** tool route → complete diagnostic task without public tokens or calculations changing.

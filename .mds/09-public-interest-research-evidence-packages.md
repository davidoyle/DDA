# Institutional and policy analysis

## What can the institution, policy, or public claim withstand?

A mandate, policy, public statement, or institutional response can appear complete until budgets, decisions, appeals, enforcement, communications, and implementation are read together. DDA structures that evidence around the decision the reader needs to make.

**Build the analysis around the decision**

## The consequential decision

A policy, legal, media, advocacy, oversight, or public-sector team may need to decide what can be stated, which mechanism needs closer review, what should be tested next, or where a conclusion must remain open.

## The controlling condition

The controlling condition is often a break in the chain between a stated mandate and an observable response. It may be a missing source, conflicting dates, an unexplained decision fork, a budget that does not match the stated mechanism, or an implementation step with no clear owner.

## The analysis

DDA builds a claim-and-source register and a chronology. It identifies what the claim says, which source supports it, what conflicts remain, and what the reader can responsibly infer. A FLAG identifies a material unknown, its consequence, and the route to resolve it.

The work can combine public-record synthesis, claim-versus-evidence analysis, decision forks, and policy mechanisms. For institutional-response questions, it can add competing hypotheses, detection lag, responsibility, and implementation analysis.

## The usable output

A scoped package can include a research question and scope memo, primary-source evidence register, chronology, actor or process map, hypothesis and falsification structure, structural gap analysis, briefing memo, publication-ready report, verification appendix, plain-language version, and correction log.

The buyer can make a narrower claim with stronger support, ask a precise institutional question, obtain a missing source, or defer a point that has not been established.

This is research and strategic analysis. It is not legal representation, privileged work, medical advice, investment advice, or a finding that an allegation is true. Sensitive personal, Indigenous, confidential, or commercially restricted material requires an agreed scope, authorization, and handling method.

# Privacy

**Last updated: September 1, 2026**

DDA respects the privacy of people who visit this website, use its diagnostic tools, or contact the practice.

## Information you provide

When you submit an inquiry, DDA may receive your name, email address, organization, message, timing, and any other information you choose to include.

Please do not send privileged, medical, financial, personal, or commercially sensitive documents through the initial contact form. DDA can arrange an appropriate transfer method if a project moves forward.

## Information produced by the website

The website and its service providers may process limited technical information needed to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the site. This may include an IP address, browser and device information, referring page, requested page, and the date and time of access.

DDA does not send contact-form text or raw diagnostic-tool inputs to analytics services.

## How DDA uses information

DDA may use information to:

- respond to an inquiry;
- assess whether a possible project is a fit;
- operate, protect, and improve the website;
- meet legal, security, and business-record obligations.

DDA does not sell personal information.

## Service providers and disclosure

DDA may use hosting, email, form-processing, security, and analytics providers to operate the site. Those providers process information for the services they supply. DDA may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the website, the practice, or another person.

## Retention and security

DDA keeps information only as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, legal obligations, security, or business records. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

## Your questions

To ask about privacy or request access to or correction of information you have provided, use the [Contact page](./16-contact.md).

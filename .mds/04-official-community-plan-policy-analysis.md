# Official Community Plan review and policy analysis

A plan can be internally coherent and still leave the delivery mechanism unresolved. DDA examines how targets, land, bylaws, servicing, infrastructure, market conditions, and statutory processes work together in practice.

## Where plans become implementation problems

An OCP review, zoning amendment, housing response, or policy package may contain a clear objective without showing which mechanism will produce it. Permitted capacity may include land that cannot be serviced, assembled, financed, or delivered in the planning horizon.

DDA separates theoretical capacity from realistic and policy-adjusted capacity. It compares policy claims with implementation evidence and makes open conditions visible before a hearing, adoption, or investment decision.

## Following policy through to delivery

For each material policy, the analysis asks what the policy says, what evidence supports the stated condition, what mechanism is expected to produce the result, and what must be true for delivery. It can connect parcel-level GIS, zoning and OCP alignment, housing evidence, servicing, hazards, infrastructure, approvals, and construction economics.

The work keeps factual findings, policy choices, and unresolved delivery questions distinct. This gives staff, council, advisers, and the public a clearer basis for scrutiny.

## Useful working products

Depending on scope, DDA may build an OCP-to-zoning crosswalk, annotated bylaw, parcel inventory, policy alignment matrix, evidence register, capacity reconciliation, policy options, engagement material, or hearing brief. The output is shaped around the live process and the decision it needs to support.

This is policy and evidence analysis. It is not a legal opinion or compliance certification. Statutory requirements and current local conditions require current review.

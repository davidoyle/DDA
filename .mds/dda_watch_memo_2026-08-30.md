# DDA Watch Memo

## Projects, positions, and open levers

**Prepared:** August 30, 2026  
**Basis:** Claude export and the DDA record available through August 29, 2026  
**Status:** Working memo; update when an external decision, client response, award, adoption, or new source changes a watch item.

## Purpose

This memo is DDA’s inventory of positions worth watching.

An item belongs here when one of four things is true:

- a party has a fixed decision or delivery clock;
- DDA has found a gap that survives the original pursuit;
- DDA holds a reusable evidence base, model, or method that another party could use;
- the item changes DDA’s commercial, credibility, or institutional position.

Each item is read on two tracks:

1. **Near track:** what can produce a live opening, paid work, a decision, or a useful contact in the next fourteen days.
2. **Compounding track:** what builds a reference, reusable asset, category, method, or institutional position beyond that window.

The memo does not treat “proposal drafted,” “work produced,” “contract awarded,” and “paid” as interchangeable. The record is strong on what DDA built and incomplete on what happened after submission.

## Watch register

### 1. Andréa Khan / AK Consulting — the validated buyer category

**Record status:** The clearest confirmed paid transaction in the record is Andréa Khan’s $1,500 payment for David’s turnaround and evidence-building work on a live engagement. The work arrived as an evidence register, but the later record identifies speed under deadline as what was purchased.

**What DDA holds:** A repeatable front-end evidence service: rapid source acquisition, classification, register construction, gap identification, and delivery in a form another consultant can use immediately.

**Gap carried by the other party:** A consultant or small firm has won work and must build the evidence base quickly. The front-end research burden is real, time-sensitive, and often not separately priced.

**Watch trigger:** New AK Consulting phase, data pull, register update, client deadline, referral, or request for maintenance/licensing.

**Near track:** Treat this as the first instance of a buyer category, not as an isolated favour. The next signal is whether the same buyer purchases another compressed evidence build or introduces a similarly situated consultant.

**Compounding track:** Productize “DDA front-end evidence build” around deadline, scope, source count, and gap register. The reusable asset is the speed-to-defensible-evidence process.

**Confidence:** High on the payment and buyer interaction; low on the complete downstream client outcome.

### 2. District of Sooke — Employment Lands Strategy

**Record status:** A substantial proposal was prepared for the District of Sooke, including a full employment-lands strategy response, team/subcontractor agreements, BC Bid Appendix B structure, and typeset proposal PDFs. The record does not confirm award.

**What DDA holds:** A complete proposal architecture, evidence base, team model, and a worked example of how DDA translates land, servicing, employment, and policy evidence into a decision document.

**Gap carried by the other party:** The District needs an employment-lands strategy that connects land supply to actual economic and servicing constraints and can survive public and procurement scrutiny.

**Watch trigger:** Award notice, clarification request, interview, staff report, contract execution, or public release of the District’s next employment-lands decision.

**Near track:** Record the outcome and any evaluator signal. A loss is still useful if the reason is known; it distinguishes qualification, procurement fit, price, and substantive method.

**Compounding track:** Preserve the proposal’s best evidence chain as a reusable employment-lands package. The team model also tests whether DDA can remain principal-led while coordinating specialist capacity.

**Confidence:** High on what was produced; unknown on award and payment.

### 3. Regional District of Bulkley-Nechako — Economic Development and Tourism

**Record status:** Proposal and substantial strategy work were produced. The record describes an integrated economic-development plan, regional tourism strategy, engagement plan, multiple revisions, SWOT work, and a 243-row evidence register. Contract status is not cleanly confirmed.

**What DDA holds:** The largest recorded regional evidence base, an integrated strategy structure, and a reusable initialization prompt for future registers.

**Gap carried by the other party:** Regional strategy work often separates economic development, tourism, community engagement, and implementation. The DDA work holds them in one evidence system and forces each recommendation toward a responsible party and action.

**Watch trigger:** Award/contract confirmation, phase approval, staff or board report, engagement schedule, strategy adoption, or request for register maintenance.

**Near track:** Resolve whether the work became a live engagement. If not, identify the narrowest paid component a regional consultant or adjacent district could authorize.

**Compounding track:** Use the 243-row register and the generalized pull-list prompt as the backbone of a licensable regional-strategy evidence product.

**Confidence:** High on the artifacts; uncertain on the external status.

**Record status:** A labour-market proposal is recorded at $41,000, with a 65 ACTUAL / 35 PROXY / 9 FLAG register. Related work includes licensing/evidence support connected to Andréa Khan and AK Consulting.

**What DDA holds:** A labour-market research architecture and a concrete demonstration of how the register travels between DDA and another consultant.

### 5. BC Ministry of Energy and Climate Solutions — LNG/fiscal model

**Record status:** RFP GS27MAN0002. DDA prepared a proposal and a 12-module LNG/natural-gas financial-model architecture with royalty, tax, scenario, sensitivity, and jurisdiction-benchmark components. A $155,000 proposal value is recorded in later material; award is unknown.

**What DDA holds:** A Crown-level financial-model proof point that is materially different from municipal planning work.

**Gap carried by the other party:** A government decision-maker needs a transparent model that connects project economics to public revenue and shows what changes under different assumptions.

**Watch trigger:** Award notice, clarification, model review, ministry procurement update, fiscal-policy change, or a comparable energy RFP.

**Near track:** Keep the model architecture ready for reuse with an energy operator, ministry-adjacent advisor, or legal/economic team. The next opportunity need not be the original ministry.

**Compounding track:** This establishes DDA as capable of high-value fiscal architecture, not only planning reports. It should remain separate from the municipal case-study stream.

**Confidence:** High on the proposal/model architecture; unknown on award, execution, and payment.

### 6. Fruitvale — OCP, zoning, and the delivery contradiction

**Record status:** The original pursuit produced a full proposal, engagement strategy, background report, zoning-bylaw work, OCP draft, First Nations consultation framework, multiple revisions, and a 91 ACTUAL / 10 PROXY / 21 FLAG register. The original contract outcome is not confirmed.

**Durable finding:** The Village’s own 2024 duplex RFP is recorded as acknowledging that builders could not deliver at the stated $300,000–$320,000 range, while the OCP process was working from a delivery assumption that did not reconcile with that evidence. The record also captures a 20-year need of 291 units against a historical delivery rate of 3.6 units per year. Vacancy across Trail/Warfield/Fruitvale is recorded as falling from 12% to 3% between 2015 and 2017, showing demand while delivery failed to respond.

**Gap carried by the other party:** The Village carries a contradiction between planned housing need and observed delivery economics. The problem remains after the procurement decision.

**What DDA holds:** A sourced, portable finding with a named consequence. It is useful to a municipality, builder, housing proponent, regional planner, lender, or advisor, depending on which side of the constraint they occupy.

**Watch trigger:** OCP adoption, zoning-bylaw adoption, public hearing, new housing/duplex procurement, council report, builder response, or a revised housing target.

**Near track:** Keep the finding alive as a position, not as a post-mortem. The next buyer may be the party trying to explain why the target will not be delivered, not the party responsible for defending the target.

**Compounding track:** Fruitvale is the model for the memo itself: a pursuit can fail while leaving DDA with a durable, multi-party evidence position.

**Confidence:** High on the existence of the work and the recorded finding; external adoption and present status are unconfirmed.

**Confidence:** High on proposal and register; unknown on award and payment.

## Durable external positions

### 15. Eskay Creek — labour constraint and buyer selection

**Record status:** DDA’s labour analysis is recorded as complete/active. The work identified a 67% pipefitter gap, a recorded $4M/day and $360M deferral exposure, a four-actor control map, and false solutions. The model was later described as potentially conservative because it did not fully price regional competition.

**Durable finding:** The issue is not simply “a labour shortage.” It is a non-substitutable trade gap tied to a project date and a regional pool competing across concurrent projects.

**Gap carried by the other party:** The operator carries a schedule risk that becomes expensive before the construction schedule visibly records it.

**What DDA holds:** A decisive-point analysis that may be more valuable to a contractor, union local, investor, Nation, or adjacent project participant than to the operator whose timeline it threatens.

**Watch trigger:** Project approval, construction/commissioning schedule, labour procurement, concurrent regional-project announcements, wage changes, or workforce-plan release.

**Near track:** Watch the seat on the other side of the constraint. The same finding can be a threat to the operator and an opportunity to the party that can close the gap.

**Compounding track:** Eskay is DDA’s strongest demonstration that truth can be commercially valuable without being welcome to the first recipient.

### 16. Baptiste / FPX Nickel — workforce and project-risk model

**Record status:** DDA produced a 13-tab labour-risk model, nine-trade failure map, delay-cost analysis, and five-risk strategic briefing for a recorded $2.97B project.

**What DDA holds:** A resource-project risk architecture that connects trades, Indigenous labour, training, housing, infrastructure, and schedule exposure.

**Watch trigger:** Project financing, environmental/permit milestones, construction schedule, workforce procurement, or regional training announcements.

**Near track:** Reuse the model for parties who need to price or mitigate a project risk rather than defend the project narrative.

**Compounding track:** Baptiste demonstrates that DDA can work at capital-project scale and produce multiple audience versions from one model.

### 17. Golden Triangle — labour, wage, and mobility system

**Record status:** The record contains a “Master Plan of the North,” wage/mobility analysis, concurrent-project pressure mapping, and trade-shortage work.

**What DDA holds:** A regional system model, not a single-project report.

**Watch trigger:** New project approvals, labour agreements, wage-package changes, camp/airstrip constraints, grid or logistics decisions, and regional project concurrency.

**Near track:** Use a new decision or deadline to select one narrow sellable register from the larger system map.

**Compounding track:** This is the resource-sector equivalent of the Fruitvale position: a body of work can generate several targeted products because the system contains several parties with different interests.

### 18. WorkSafeBC — repricing, surplus, and institutional design

**Record status:** The record contains a substantial reform analysis, employer diagnostics, rate and surplus work, constitutional challenge material, and public-interest documents. Some findings are framed as scenarios or analytical conclusions, not verified current institutional facts.

**What DDA holds:** A deep institutional case library and a product/tool family.

**Gap carried by the other party:** Employers, associations, workers, advocates, and legal teams need to understand how rate, surplus, claims, adjudication, and policy decisions interact rather than reading each document separately.

**Watch trigger:** Rate-setting changes, surplus deployment, appeals data, policy changes, tribunal decisions, legislative action, or new employer exposure.

**Near track:** Watch for a decision with a named date and a party that bears a measurable cost. The general reform thesis is too broad by itself.

**Compounding track:** Keep WorkSafeBC as the originating case for DDA’s method and as the research base for Workers Toolkit/CRLS, while firewalling commercial client work.

## Internal assets to watch

### 23. The Data Lexicon and formal DDA method

**Watch because:** This is the product spine. Every new engagement should either strengthen the shared register or show a distinct problem-shape architecture.

**Next update:** Maintain examples of what each method catches: Fruitvale’s delivery contradiction, Eskay’s non-substitutable trade gap, PST’s reconciliation-to-official-number approach, and teacher attrition’s category separation.

**Risk:** A flat list of principles turns the method into marketing copy. The record shows that DDA’s actual method changes with the shape of the problem.

### 24. Proposal and evidence-library reuse

**Watch because:** DDA has a large sunk library of proposals, registers, work plans, fee schedules, AI disclosures, contract language, and document components. The library lowers production cost and increases speed.

**Next update:** For each asset, record jurisdiction, source vintage, decision unit, unresolved FLAGS, last use, and what can be licensed or reused.

**Risk:** Reuse can become production for its own sake. A reusable asset matters when it shortens the path to a buyer with a deadline.

### 25. Employer diagnostic platform and website

**Watch because:** The platform turns DDA’s analysis into self-serve or lead-generation tools, while the website explains the method and routes prospects.

**Recorded components:** WCB rate, PST, mental-health claims, multi-province rate comparison, claims suppression, experience rating, surplus run-down, and additional scoped tools. An internal Engagement Command Center is recorded with Flags, Deliverables, Evidence, Finance, Contracts, Calendar, and Stakeholders.

**Next update:** Record which tools are live, which are demos, which produce a qualified contact, and which have a paid delivery attached.

**Risk:** Tool count and deployment status vary in the record. Do not use “seven,” “eight,” or “more than seven” as a current fact until reconciled.

### 26. Workers Toolkit / DDA / CRLS architecture

**Watch because:** The three-entity structure is intended to convert worker evidence into DDA analysis and litigation support while preserving legal, financial, and research independence.

**Next update:** Confirm execution, not just drafting, of the DDA–Toolkit service agreement, IP schedule, conflict review, charity status, CRLS trust-account setup, and related-party controls.

**Risk:** The Project contains extensive draft governance and financial instruments. Draft architecture is not evidence of legal implementation.

### 27. Corrections, refusals, and credibility events

**Watch because:** The record’s strongest evidence of how DDA works may be what David corrects, refuses, or loses because the finding is difficult to hear.

**Recorded examples:** public correction of superseded data, keeping a comparison open rather than forcing it, the Central Coast correction after the bid, and Eskay’s finding being unwelcome to the operator.

**Next update:** Maintain a short correction/refusal log with the original claim, the correction or refusal, the affected audience, and what the event proves about DDA.

**Compounding track:** These events are not private process notes. They are evidence of research independence and adversarial reliability.

## Operating conclusion

DDA should watch positions, not just projects.

A project is worth watching when it contains a party with a deadline, a gap that party has not priced, and an evidence object DDA can carry into the decision. Fruitvale is the clearest municipal example. Eskay is the clearest resource example. Andréa’s $1,500 transaction is the clearest commercial example.

The record shows three kinds of value:

- **decision value:** a live party needs a gap closed;
- **asset value:** DDA has already built a register, model, or method that survives the original pursuit;
- **proof value:** a correction, refusal, or difficult finding demonstrates that DDA’s independence is real.

The next memo revision should not add more projects by default. It should add the missing dates, owners, decisions, and consequences that turn this inventory into a working instrument.

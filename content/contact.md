# Talk to DDA

## Show us what you are working on

A few sentences are enough. Send the report, RFP, project, dataset, strategy, public link, or open question. DDA will review it and start with a conversation where there is a fit.

### What to send

Tell us what is happening, what needs a decision, and what the current explanation has not resolved. Add any dates that limit the work. Send a link or a short description of the material. You do not need a finished scope.

### What to keep out of the first message

Do not send privileged, personal, medical, or commercially sensitive documents in an initial inquiry. Identify the material first. We will agree on scope, authorization, and handling before anyone shares it.

### What happens next

An inquiry does not create an engagement. DDA reads your message and replies. If there is a fit, we start with a conversation about the problem. We agree on scope and terms before any work begins.
